### CSF 801 – Design & Analysis of Algorithms
- **Concepts**: Algorithm definition, problem-solving, pseudocode, flowcharts:contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}.

### CSC 806 – Research Methods
- **Proposal**: Problem statement, objectives, methods, timeline, references:contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}.

### CSA 804 – Responsible & Explainable AI
- **XAI Tools**: LIME, SHAP, PDP/ICE, Anchors, CAM:contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}.  
- **Models**: Interpretable (decision trees, linear), post-hoc explanations.

- **Practice**: Apply shap/lime/sklearn on Iris/Titanic datasets.  

### MAT 801 – Mathematics for AI
- **Linear Algebra**: Vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, diagonalization.  
- **SVD**: Dimensionality reduction, NLP, compression.  
- **Calculus**: Limits, derivatives, gradients, chain rule.  
- **Multivariate**: Jacobians, Hessians, optimization:contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}.  
- **Practice**: Use numpy/sympy; visualize gradient descent with matplotlib.

## 📝 Quick Flashcards (Revision)

- **Research Proposal**: Problem, objectives, literature review, methods, timeline, refs.  
- **Lit Review Purpose**: Identify gaps, justify study.  
- **XAI**: Makes AI interpretable & transparent.  
- **LIME**: Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations.  
- **SHAP**: Explains feature contribution.  
- **Counterfactuals**: Show minimal input change needed to alter prediction.  
- **Big-O**: Upper bound of algorithm runtime.  
- **Greedy Algorithm Example**: Kruskal’s / Activity selection.  
- **Dynamic Programming**: Break into overlapping subproblems.  
- **NP-complete**: No known poly-time algorithm; all NP reduces to it.  
- **Eigenvector**: Vector scaled by a transformation.  
- **SVD**: Dimensionality reduction, noise reduction, compression.  
- **Gradient**: Vector of partial derivatives pointing steepest ascent:contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}.
