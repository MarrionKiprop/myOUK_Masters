## 🧭 Semester 1 Study Guide

### CSF 801 – Design & Analysis of Algorithms
- Problem-solving, pseudocode, flowcharts  
- Divide & Conquer, Greedy, Dynamic Programming  
- Time/space complexity, Big-O/Θ/Ω, Master Theorem  
- Sorting (Merge, Quick, Counting), DP (Fibonacci, LCS, Knapsack), Greedy (Activity Selection, Kruskal, Huffman)  
- NP-completeness, amortized analysis  

### CSC 806 – Research Methods
- Research paradigms: qualitative, quantitative, mixed methods  
- Proposal components: problem, objectives, methodology, timeline, references  
- Literature review: strategies & gap analysis  
- Data collection: sampling, tools, reliability  
- Academic integrity & ethics  

### CSA 804 – Responsible & Explainable AI
- Interpretable models (decision trees, linear, rule-based)  
- Explanation tools: LIME, SHAP, PDP/ICE, Anchors, CAM  
- Responsible AI: fairness, transparency, accountability  
- Risks of black-box AI  

### MAT 801 – Mathematics for AI
- Linear algebra: vectors, matrices, eigenvalues/eigenvectors  
- SVD: dimensionality reduction, compression  
- Calculus: derivatives, gradients, chain rule  
- Multivariate: Jacobians, Hessians, optimization  
- Gradient descent visualization

## 📝 Quick Flashcards

- **Research Proposal** → Problem, objectives, lit review, methodology, timeline, refs  
- **Lit Review** → Analyze research, identify gaps, justify study  
- **XAI** → Interpretability & transparency  
- **LIME** → Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations  
- **SHAP** → Explains feature contribution  
- **Counterfactuals** → Smallest input change to alter prediction  
- **Big-O** → Upper bound runtime growth  
- **Greedy Example** → Kruskal’s / Activity Selection  
- **Dynamic Programming** → Overlapping subproblems + optimal substructure  
- **NP-complete** → No known polynomial-time solution  
- **Eigenvector** → Scaled by transformation  
- **SVD** → Dimensionality reduction, compression  
- **Gradient** → Vector of partial derivatives
